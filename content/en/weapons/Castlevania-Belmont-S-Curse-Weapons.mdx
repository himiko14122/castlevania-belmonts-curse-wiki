Before you commit to a build, it helps to know how the seven families compare. Each category has its own **base damage range**, **weight class**, and **signature combo path**, and the in-game stat sheet surfaces those numbers the moment you hover an icon. The table below summarizes the families using data reported by players during the pre-release beta and cross-referenced with the [KONAMI official site](https://www.konami.com/games/castlevania/belmonts_curse/us/en-us/).

Every Castlevania: Belmont's Curse weapon list entry falls into exactly one of these buckets, which keeps loadout theorycrafting approachable even if you only have ten minutes between save points. Light weapons trade raw damage for **combo chaining**, while heavy weapons let you posture-break tougher enemies during the late-game Paris gauntlets.

### How Weight Shapes Combat

Weight is the silent stat that decides how a weapon actually feels. Light weapons (whips, daggers, firearms) let Rose cancel into a dash on frame 1, which makes them ideal when the screen fills with projectile spam. Heavy weapons (great swords, axes) lock you in for longer but unlock **armor-piercing follow-ups** that skip an enemy's damage phase. The Castlevania: Belmont's Curse weapon categories all share the same stamina economy, so switching families mid-run only changes the timing, not the resource pool.

## Swords and Great Swords: The Reliable Backbone

### Standard Swords

Swords are the **default category** and the first family Rose picks up in the Parisian prologue. They trade peak damage for the cleanest input buffer, which means new players will live here longer than expected. The tier-relevant picks include the **Alucard Sword**, the **Belmont Saber**, and the **Crimson Edge**, each tuned for a slightly different pacing window.

| Sword | Base ATK | Speed Tier | Notable Trait |
|-------|----------|------------|---------------|
| Alucard Sword | 42 | Fast | Bonus damage vs. vampires |
| Belmont Saber | 36 | Medium | Built-in Arcana gain on hit |
| Crimson Edge | 48 | Medium | Lifesteal on combo finisher |

The Alucard Sword is the standout for players who main Arcana builds because every third swing channels a small chunk of magic, which keeps your tarot hand topped off. According to the [Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castlevania:_Belmont%27s_Curse), the Alucard style returns as a pre-order costume, but the sword itself is available in-game as a mid-Paris reward — community testing confirms the drop is guaranteed on the second cathedral run, not random.

### Great Swords

Great Swords turn Rose into a slower but harder-hitting version of herself. The **Ironclad Greatblade** and the **Crimson Greatsword** headline the category, both accessible once you reach the inner castle. They reward patience — a fully charged swing will stagger most mid-bosses on contact, which makes them excellent against the armored Paris guard captains that shrug off lighter hits.

Because the charge window is long, you'll want to pair these with relics that grant **i-frames on guard**, a synergy that the [Castlevania: Belmont's Curse relic effects guide](/en/relics/castlevania-belmont-s-curse-relics/) breaks down in detail. When you do land a charge, expect roughly 1.5x the listed base damage plus a stagger that buys two free follow-ups.

## Whips, Daggers, and Firearms: The Speed Trio

### Whips

Whips are the **signature Belmont weapon family**, and the game leans into the legacy by giving Rose the **Vampire Killer** as a story reward. Whip combos hit multiple times per swing, which makes them perfect for clearing crowds of smaller undead without ever committing to a heavy finisher. The Vampire Killer in particular chains four hits before a knockback, and the knockback itself is a damage window because enemies bounce into walls.

| Whip | Base ATK | Hit Count | Range Bonus |
|------|----------|-----------|-------------|
| Leather Whip | 24 | 3 | +0.5 tile |
| Chain Whip | 30 | 3 | +1 tile |
| Vampire Killer | 45 | 4 | +1.5 tiles |
| Thornlash | 38 | 4 | +1 tile, poison |

The Thornlash is the late-game alternative for poison-focused builds, and it interacts well with Arcana cards that spread status effects. If you want a fuller ranking of the whip family's best-in-slot, the [Castlevania: Belmont's Curse weapon tier list](/en/weapons/castlevania-belmont-s-curse-weapon-tier-list/) goes deep on stat breakpoints.

### Daggers and Firearms

Daggers and firearms share the lightest weight class, but they serve opposite jobs. Daggers are **rushdown melee** — they let you dash through an enemy and strike on the other side, which is the cleanest answer to enemies with charge attacks. The **Parrying Dagger** in particular adds a guard counter that staggers most standard mobs, making it a sleeper pick for players who prefer reactive play.

Firearms, by contrast, are **ranged pressure tools**. The **Belmont Revolver** and the **Rose Iron** let you proc Arcana cards at a distance and break enemy shields from outside their aggro radius. They're weak in pure damage terms, but in a game with seven weapon categories, having a tool that closes distance is its own kind of power.

## Axes and Spears: The Niche Power Picks

### Axes

Axes are the heaviest of the seven weapon categories, and they trade every other stat for **raw single-target damage**. The **Crimson Cleaver** is the iconic example, with a wind-up that looks slow but punishes anything it connects with. Axes are best reserved for boss attempts where you need a single big number, because their stamina cost makes them inefficient against trash packs.

| Axe | Base ATK | Charge Bonus | Stagger on Hit |
|-----|----------|--------------|----------------|
| Iron Cleaver | 55 | +30% | Yes |
| Crimson Cleaver | 68 | +45% | Yes |
| Bonecleaver | 72 | +20% | Yes, +bleed |

The Bonecleaver is a community-favorite because the bleed status stacks with Arcana cards, but its drop location is in the deepest part of the inner castle, so plan your route accordingly. The bleed effect rewards patient play, and community testing recommends pairing it with an Arcana card that keys off status damage to multiply the payoff over a long fight.

### Mid-Range Spears

Spears are the **mid-range control option**, ideal for line formations and corridor fights. The **Belmont Halberd** and the **Crimson Spear** are the two standout pieces. Their poke attack has the longest reach of any melee family, which lets you keep threats at a comfortable distance while still applying stagger pressure. Spears are also the most forgiving category for new players, because the tip-first hitbox means mistimed swings still land.

Because they sit between speed and weight, spears also synergize well with [Arcana card builds](/en/arcana/castlevania-belmont-s-curse-arcana/) that trigger on the third consecutive hit — spears naturally deliver three-hit strings faster than any other family. That rhythm also makes them the safest family for corridor ambushes, where retreating between pokes is often worth more than a faster weapon's raw output.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many weapon categories are in Castlevania: Belmont's Curse?**

There are seven weapon categories: swords, great swords, whips, daggers, spears, axes, and firearms. Each family has its own weight class, combo path, and stat curve, so the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse weapon categories system lets you mix and match rather than commit to one path.

**What is the best early weapon in Castlevania: Belmont's Curse?**

The Belmont Saber is the most reliable early weapon because its medium speed, balanced damage, and built-in Arcana gain make it useful from the Paris streets through the first castle wing. It also unlocks as part of the tutorial, so you can start refining your build immediately.

**Can you use the Vampire Killer whip in Castlevania: Belmont's Curse?**

Yes. The Vampire Killer is a story-reward whip that becomes available after the second major boss. It hits four times per swing, has the longest reach in the whip family, and is widely considered one of the best weapons in Castlevania: Belmont's Curse for crowd control.

**Do weapons affect Arcana card triggers in Castlevania: Belmont's Curse?**

Weapons affect Arcana card triggers through combo speed and hit count. Light weapons like daggers and whips trigger on-hit Arcana faster, while heavy weapons like great swords and axes trigger charge-based Arcana more reliably. The exact trigger window is shown on each card's tooltip.

**Where do you find late-game weapons in Castlevania: Belmont's Curse?**

Late-game weapons drop in the inner castle's deepest chambers and from optional mini-bosses hidden off the main path. The Bonecleaver axe and the Thornlash whip are two confirmed late-game picks, both gated behind exploration rather than story progression, so check every wall for breakable seams.
