## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse item list reset on New Game+?**

Yes, the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse item list fully resets on New Game+, but any costumes, Relics, and Arcana cards you've unlocked stay unlocked because they live in a separate save slot. Weapons and consumables restart, while cosmetics and meta-progression persist.

**Where do I find Cursed chests in the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse treasure locations map?**

Cursed chests spawn in three regions: the Castle approach, the Castle interior, and the late-game Catacombs. They always appear near a mini-boss arena, and they sometimes replace a Silver chest after the mini-boss is defeated. Their purple lid color is the visual cue.

**Can I miss a costume in the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse costumes list forever?**

Only one costume, **Maria's Dress**, is missable in a single run. If you skip the Cathedral dialogue with the friendly NPC before Act 3, the costume becomes unavailable until New Game+. Every other costume is re-obtainable from the weekly shop or boss replays.

**How do I tell the difference between a real chest and a mimic in the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse chests guide?**

A real Cursed chest has a faint purple glow, while a mimic has no glow and only spawns when you turn your back to the chest for more than three seconds. According to community data, the safe opening tactic is to face the chest and strike once without releasing the attack button.

**What is the rarest item in the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse rare items pool?**

The rarest pickup in the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse rare items list is the **Mythic Relic of the House Belmont**, which drops only from the final boss on Hard difficulty. It grants +10% damage to all weapon categories and is the single most-coveted item in the entire Castlevania: Belmont's Curse item list.
