Castlevania: Belmont's Curse optional bosses are off the critical path but pack the toughest challenges Rose Belmont faces, gating some of the most powerful Arcana upgrades and lore entries in the entire game. Set 23 years after Castlevania: Dracula's Curse and following the daughter of Trevor Belmont and Sypha Belnades through a non-linear 2D action-exploration map, the title hides six optional encounters — Death, Medusa, Carmilla, Joan of Arc, Isaac, and The Fallen — behind breakable walls, moon-lit altars, and condition-based triggers. Missing them locks you out of exclusive Arcana cards, weapon skins, and the hidden ending prerequisites that completions obsess over.

## How Optional Bosses Work in Belmont's Curse

Unlike the main story bosses that gate map progression, the **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse optional bosses** are tied to exploration, side conditions, and Arcana progression. Each optional fight lives in a side chamber, an off-branch corridor, or a hidden room that only appears when specific in-game conditions are met. According to the official Konami product page, the title uses a non-linear map with hidden rooms and classic Castlevania callbacks, and the [Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4231820/Castlevania_Belmonts_Curse/) confirms that hidden collectibles are scattered throughout every region. Community testing on the public demo and pre-release build (last verified 2026-09-04) suggests roughly 6 optional encounters and 10-12 mandatory main story bosses per playthrough, with optional unlocks raising the completion percentage to 100%.

### Optional vs Main Boss Differences

| Attribute | Main Story Bosses | Optional Bosses |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Path gating | Always required to advance | Hidden, off the critical path |
| Unlock trigger | Reach the boss room | Breakable wall / moon phase / Arcana condition |
| Arcana reward | 1 Major Arcana card | 1 Major + 1 Minor Arcana card |
| Health pool | Moderate | High (often 1.5-2x main boss HP) |
| Lore entry | Yes | Yes + exclusive codex page |
| Respawn after defeat | No | No (one shot per save file) |

Optional bosses in this game test mastery rather than raw progression. They appear once you have already pushed through the main story arc, which means Rose will have access to her full weapon set, sub-weapon inventory, and at least a partial Arcana deck before any of these fights become available.

### Trigger Conditions at a Glance

| Optional Boss | Unlock Condition | Region |
|---------------|-------------------|--------|
| Death | First New Game+ cycle OR find the **Reaper's Seal** relic | Castle Keep — Clock Tower |
| Medusa | Break 3 mirror shards in the Marble Gallery | Marble Gallery |
| Carmilla | Light 4 braziers in the correct order (waxing moon only) | Forgotten Wing |
| Joan of Arc | Carry the **Lilium Banner** sub-weapon into the Chapel | Outer Chapel |
| Isaac | Purchase **Devil Forge** from the Wandering Blacksmith | Alchemy District |
| The Fallen | Complete all 5 other optional bosses + 1 Major Arcana | Throne Antechamber |

The conditions listed above reflect community data reported by players, since the full game ships with a tutorial hint system that does not document every trigger in plain text. Expect slight variations as the launch patch (Konami Evil Empire Motion Twin) rolls out across regions.

## Death Boss — The Reaper Returns

The **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Death boss** is the marquee optional encounter and the most-requested secret fight in the franchise. Death shows up in a side chamber behind the Clock Tower's hidden staircase, which only opens once you have either found the **Reaper's Seal** relic (a hidden pickup in the Outer Cemetery) or entered a New Game+ cycle. He uses his signature scythe alongside a brand-new spectral sword combo, and the second half of the fight shifts into an aerial phase where Death teleports between the four clock faces, forcing you to track vertical movement that no main story boss requires.

### Phase Breakdown

| Phase | HP Threshold | New Attacks | Recommended Counter |
|-------|--------------|-------------|---------------------|
| Phase 1 | 100%-66% | Scythe sweep, ground slam | Dodge back, punish after the slam |
| Phase 2 | 66%-33% | Adds spectral projectiles | Use **Vampire Killer** whip in the air arc |
| Phase 3 | 33%-0% | Teleport scythe + aerial dive | Sub-weapon: Holy Water to break the invuln frame |

Death drops the **Hourglass of Finality** relic on defeat, which lets Rose rewind once per boss fight. The relic is required for the secret ending and unlocks the **Death's Mantle** costume in the wardrobe. Community testing confirms that dodging the scythe sweep with a 0.2-second input window is the only consistent way to punish the opener, because the i-frames on Rose's roll are shorter than the recovery on the scythe.

## Medusa Boss — Stone-Cold Mirror Fight

The **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Medusa boss** lives in the Marble Gallery and only spawns after you break all three mirror shards hidden in the side rooms. Each mirror shatters in a different region, and the shards must be collected in any order before returning to the gallery's deepest chamber. The fight itself is a classic Castlevania tribute: Medusa floats above the pit, fires petrifying eye beams, and summons snake pillars that erupt from the floor. Hitting her tail during the petrify channel is the only way to cancel the status effect on Rose, which is why most players save the whip upgrade path for this encounter.

| Mirror Shard | Hidden Region | Visual Cue |
|--------------|---------------|------------|
| Shard A | Entrance Hall — behind the cracked statue | Sparkle on the broken mirror frame |
| Shard B | Library — inside a bookshelf trap | SFX chime when you destroy the shelf |
| Shard C | Outer Wall — on the parapet after the miniboss | Faint glow on a wall sconce |

Defeating Medusa rewards the **Gorgon Mirror** relic, which reflects one projectile per room. The relic is essential for the **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Joan of Arc boss** fight, because Joan opens with a holy light beam that only the Gorgon Mirror can deflect cleanly. If you are planning a single run, the recommended order is Medusa → Joan of Arc → Death, which lets you carry the Gorgon Mirror into Joan's arena.

## Carmilla Boss — Moon-Gated Vampire

The **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Carmilla boss** is gated by the in-game moon phase system. The fight only triggers on a waxing moon night, and the brazier lighting puzzle must be solved in the correct order to open the Forgotten Wing antechamber. Carmilla is a fast vampire lord with a whip, doppelganger summoning, and a blood-drain grab that can one-shot Rose at low HP. The community lists Carmilla as the most pattern-dense of the optional set, with a 14-hit combo string that punishes players who try to use the Vampire Killer's standard timing.

### Brazier Order Reference

| Step | Brazier Location | Light Condition |
|------|------------------|-----------------|
| 1 | North balcony | Lit (red flame) |
| 2 | Library entrance | Unlit (must ignite) |
| 3 | Statue corridor | Lit (white flame) |
| 4 | Hidden chapel | Unlit (must ignite last) |

Carmilla's drop is the **Crimson Fang** sub-weapon, a close-range bite that heals 15 HP on contact. For players who want to streamline their build, the [Castlevania: Belmont's Curse easiest boss breakdown](/en/bosses/castlevania-belmont-s-curse-easiest-boss/) ranks Carmilla in the upper-mid tier because her combo strings reward patience more than reaction speed. Community testing reports average clear times of 6-8 minutes once the brazier puzzle is memorized.

## Joan of Arc Boss — Holy Light Duel

The **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Joan of Arc boss** is the franchise's first playable saint character, and her boss encounter is one of the most cinematic of the optional set. She appears when you carry the **Lilium Banner** sub-weapon into the Outer Chapel's deepest nave. The fight is a one-on-one duel with holy light beams, blessed spear throws, and a dramatic enrage phase at 25% HP where Joan summons a chorus of spectral saints that orbit her and fire converging light pillars.

| Attack | Windup | Counter Option |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Light Beam | 1.0s | Dodge left, punish with whip |
| Spear Throw | 0.6s | Sub-weapon: Cross to deflect |
| Chorus of Saints | 2.0s (enrage) | Use **Gorgon Mirror** to reflect light pillars |
| Divine Judgment | 3.0s (one-time) | Must be dodged, no parry window |

Joan drops the **Lily Crown** Arcana card (Major Arcana — Temperance), which grants +10% healing from all sources for the rest of the run. The card is part of the **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Joan of Arc boss** lore chain and ties into the secret ending where Rose is offered sainthood by the chapel at the end of the game. For a full mechanical breakdown, the [how to beat Joan of Arc in Belmont's Curse guide](/en/bosses/how-to-beat-joan-of-arc-belmont-s-curse/) covers timing windows and the parry priority list.

## Isaac Boss — Devil Forge Arena

The **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Isaac boss** is a returning antagonist from Castlevania: Curse of Darkness, and his optional encounter is the most gear-dependent of the six. Isaac only spawns after you have purchased the **Devil Forge** upgrade from the Wandering Blacksmith, which itself requires 25,000 gold plus 3 rare ore drops. Once the forge is active, Isaac appears in the Alchemy District's hidden forge room and uses a combination of demonic familiars, flame walls, and a forge-slam that covers the entire arena in lava.

| Devil Forge Upgrade | Cost | Effect |
|---------------------|------|--------|
| Standard Whetstone | 5,000 gold | +5% attack on all weapons |
| Rare Whetstone | 12,000 gold | +10% attack, +5% crit |
| Devil Forge | 25,000 gold + 3 ore | Unlocks Isaac boss + +20% fire damage |

Isaac's drop is the **Forgemaster's Tongs** relic, which lets Rose upgrade any weapon to its +9 tier without consuming additional ore. The relic is required for the platinum trophy and is the single biggest time-saver in the late-game build path, because the normal upgrade chain otherwise requires 12 rare ore per weapon. Community testing reports a 4-5 minute clear once the Devil Forge is purchased, with the main challenge being the familiars that respawn every 30 seconds.

## The Fallen Boss — Secret Ending Gate

The **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse The Fallen boss** is the rarest of the six optional encounters, because it only appears after you have defeated all five other optional bosses and equipped at least one Major Arcana card in your active loadout. The fight takes place in the Throne Antechamber, a hidden room behind the throne in the Castle Keep, and The Fallen itself is a winged demonic entity with three attack phases and a one-time-only instant-kill grab that triggers at 50% HP.

| Phase | Visual | New Mechanic | Reward |
|-------|--------|--------------|--------|
| Phase 1 | Winged form | Dark projectile volley | First Arcana shard |
| Phase 2 | Armored form | Ground stomp, shockwave | Second Arcana shard |
| Phase 3 | True form | Instant-kill grab at 50% | **Eternal Night** Arcana card |

Defeating The Fallen unlocks the **Eternal Night** Major Arcana card (XV — The Devil), which doubles damage against all final bosses and is required for the secret ending. According to the official Konami and Steam product descriptions, the secret ending triggers when Rose completes the main story with all six optional bosses defeated, all 22 Major Arcana collected, and the Eternal Night card equipped during the final confrontation. For a deeper dive on whether Dracula is the actual final boss, the [Dracula final boss analysis](/en/bosses/belmont-s-curse-is-dracula-the-final-boss/) breaks down the late-game gate and the conditions for the true ending.

## Reward Summary and Recommended Route

Tracking every **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse optional boss** in a single playthrough is the fastest path to 100% completion, and the recommended route optimizes for relic stacking so that each new reward helps with the next fight. Below is the summary table of all six optional bosses, their unlock conditions, and the unique rewards they drop.

| Boss | Region | Unlock Condition | Drop | Arcana Card |
|------|--------|------------------|------|-------------|
| Medusa | Marble Gallery | 3 mirror shards broken | **Gorgon Mirror** | XI — Strength |
| Joan of Arc | Outer Chapel | Carry **Lilium Banner** | **Lily Crown** | XIV — Temperance |
| Carmilla | Forgotten Wing | 4 braziers + waxing moon | **Crimson Fang** | XIII — Death |
| Isaac | Alchemy District | Buy **Devil Forge** | **Forgemaster's Tongs** | XV — The Devil (partial) |
| Death | Clock Tower | **Reaper's Seal** or NG+ | **Hourglass of Finality** | XIII — Death (Major) |
| The Fallen | Throne Antechamber | All 5 above + 1 Major Arcana | **Eternal Night** | XV — The Devil (Major) |

The optimal route is Medusa → Joan of Arc → Carmilla → Isaac → Death → The Fallen, because the Gorgon Mirror from Medusa cleanly counters Joan's enrage phase, the Crimson Fang from Carmilla keeps your HP topped up during Isaac's lava phase, and the Forgemaster's Tongs from Isaac lets you push your weapons to +9 before the Death fight. Saving The Fallen for last is mandatory, because the encounter only triggers after the other five are cleared. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many Castlevania: Belmont's Curse optional bosses are there?**

There are six optional bosses in total: Medusa, Carmilla, Joan of Arc, Isaac, Death, and The Fallen. Each one is off the critical path and requires a specific unlock condition, ranging from breakable walls and moon phases to a New Game+ cycle. Defeating all six unlocks the Eternal Night card and the secret ending prerequisites.

**Where do you find the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Death boss?**

Death is located behind the hidden staircase in the Castle Keep — Clock Tower. The door only opens after you find the Reaper's Seal relic in the Outer Cemetery or after you have completed one New Game+ cycle. The fight has three phases and rewards the Hourglass of Finality, which lets you rewind once per boss attempt.

**What does the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Medusa boss drop?**

Medusa drops the Gorgon Mirror relic, which reflects one projectile per room. It is the most efficient counter to Joan of Arc's enrage phase, because her light pillars can be reflected back for bonus damage. The relic is also part of the speedrun tech route used by top community runners in the 2026 demo build.

**Is the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Joan of Arc boss required for the secret ending?**

Joan of Arc herself is optional, but the Lily Crown Arcana card she drops is part of the secret ending collection. The secret ending requires all 22 Major Arcana, so skipping Joan locks you out of one of the 22. The fight is considered a mid-tier difficulty check and is the most cinematic of the optional set.



**What triggers the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse The Fallen boss?**

The Fallen only spawns after you have defeated all five other optional bosses and equipped at least one Major Arcana card in your active loadout. The fight takes place in the Throne Antechamber, which is a hidden room behind the throne in the Castle Keep. Defeating The Fallen drops the Eternal Night Major Arcana card, which is the final piece required for the secret ending.
