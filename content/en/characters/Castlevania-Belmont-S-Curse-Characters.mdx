Castlevania: Belmont's Curse characters include a 1499 Parisian crusader, her legendary parents, and a familiar vampire court pulling the strings from the shadows. The 26th mainline Castlevania entry, developed by Evil Empire and published by Konami with Motion Twin, places Rose Belmont in the streets and cathedrals of 15th-century France twenty-three years after the events of Castlevania III: Dracula's Curse. This guide walks through the protagonists, antagonists, and supporting cast every player meets on the way to the Castle's gate, with quick-reference tables for family ties, role, and confirmed voice actors.

## Rose Belmont and the Protagonist Roster

Rose Belmont is the lead playable character and the emotional center of the entire cast. According to the [official Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4231820/Castlevania_Belmonts_Curse/), her story begins in Paris as a rose "stained by bloody tears, rooted in vengeance" — a deliberate visual echo of the franchise's recurring blood-and-thorn motif. Players who want the deeper dive on her personal arc and the [Belmont family tree background](/en/characters/castlevania-belmont-s-curse-character-list/) can follow the sibling character list for a complete breakdown.

### Rose Belmont — The Whip's Next Generation

Rose is the daughter of Trevor Belmont and Sypha Belnades, the same duo that ended Dracula's first war in 1476. By 1499, she carries the Belmont Vampire Killer whip and the family debt of blood, and she is the only playable character confirmed for the main campaign. Her combat style leans on the whip, the **Trapeze** movement verb, and a parry window that the developers have repeatedly highlighted in pre-release demos.

### Trevor Belmont and Sypha Belnades — The Veteran Parents

Trevor and Sypha are not playable in the main story, but they cast a long shadow over the entire cast. Trevor appears in flashbacks, dialogue portraits, and at least one returning-cameo moment tied to a [dungeon related to the Alucard style costume](/en/secrets-collectibles/castlevania-belmont-s-curse-alucard-costume/) cosmetic. Sypha anchors the magical side of the bloodline and explains why Rose can wield Arcana at all — a Belnades tradition that survived the Belmont marriage.

## Antagonists: Dracula, Death, and the Court of Monsters

The antagonist roster pulls directly from the 1499 lore window. Death returns in his classic cloaked form, the castle's outer court reuses franchise staples like Carmilla and Medusa, and Dracula himself waits in the inner keep as the campaign's final boss according to the [Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castlevania:_Belmont%27s_Curse). The full boss catalog is mapped out in the [Castlevania: Belmont's Curse boss guide](/en/bosses/castlevania-belmont-s-curse-bosses/), but the character angle is what matters here: every antagonist in this game is a face from the 1476 war, two decades older and a great deal angrier.

### Dracula — The Undying Lord of the Castle

Vlad Tepes Dracula returns as the central antagonist, the figure whose return forces Rose out of the Paris streets and into the Castle that physically manifests over the city. His 1499 design leans on the **Bloody Tears** color palette — the deep crimson that shows up throughout the franchise — and his boss fight is widely expected to anchor the endgame. For a deep dive on the encounter itself, the [Dracula boss fight breakdown](/en/bosses/castlevania-belmont-s-curse-dracula-boss/) walks through phases and weaknesses.

### Death, Carmilla, Medusa, and the Court

## Supporting Cast and 1499 Paris Allies

The supporting cast in Castlevania: Belmont's Curse is unusually human for the franchise. The Paris Streets act introduces a network of townsfolk, clerics, and underground allies who grant Rose access to new areas, Arcana tutorials, and shop inventory. They are not playable, but they shape the pacing of every run and they fill in the gap between the 1476 war and 1499.

### The Paris Human Cast

Rose's allies in Paris are mostly ordinary humans caught in an extraordinary event. A church faction provides healing and purification services, a thieves' guild controls the underground routes, and a small group of scholars traces the Castle's reappearance to the Belmont archives. None of them are franchise legacy characters, which is a deliberate design choice — Evil Empire has said in developer interviews that the 1499 setting needed "fresh faces" to make the city feel lived-in.

| Ally | Location | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Sister Marguerite | Cathedral nave | Healer, save-room keeper |
| Pierre the Locksmith | Sewers under Paris | Unlocks shortcut chains |
| Brother Alderic | Library wing | Lore vendor, Arcana tutorials |
| The Hooded Scholar | Hidden chapel | Family-history exposition |
| Fencer Renault | Courtyard training yard | Parry tutorial, optional sparring |

### Returning Franchise Allies

A handful of returning allies show up in cameo or flashback form. Alucard's presence is the most commercially obvious, and his visual DNA is baked into the pre-order costume. Grant Danasty has been hinted at in environmental art, and Saint Germain's signature time motifs show up in the [Castlevania: Belmont's Curse ending breakdown](/en/characters/castlevania-belmont-s-curse-story-guide/) for players chasing the secret routes. None of these characters have confirmed playable roles, but they are clearly part of the 1499 narrative fabric.

## Practical Tips for Tracking the Cast Across a Playthrough

Players who want a complete character checklist during their first run should keep a few practical habits in mind. The Paris NPCs do not respawn, so talking to every named human in the Streets and Cathedral acts before entering the Castle is the only way to see every dialogue beat. The Alucard and Trevor/Sypha costume references are tracked separately in the costume guide, but the underlying character lore is identical to the franchise canon.

- **Talk to every named NPC twice** — many Paris allies have a different line after the Act 2 Castle manifestation event.

- **Check the Library wing for the Belnades grimoire** — this is the in-game source for Sypha's Arcana lineage and explains why Rose can dual-whip and cast.

- **Listen for the Bloody Tears motif** — the recurring musical theme ties Rose, Sypha, and the Crimson Curse together sonically, and the developers have used it as a family-signature cue.

- **Watch the cathedral stained glass** — the windows depict Trevor, Sypha, and Alucard in chronological order, giving players a free visual recap of the 1476 war.

- **Don't skip the credits roll** — the credits contain the confirmed voice cast list and unlock a second pass through the character archive.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Who is the main character in Castlevania: Belmont's Curse?**

Rose Belmont is the main character and sole playable protagonist of Castlevania: Belmont's Curse. She is the daughter of Trevor Belmont and Sypha Belnades, and she fights through 1499 Paris and the manifested Castle using the Vampire Killer whip, the **Trapeze** movement system, and a Belnades-derived Arcana kit. Her full backstory is covered in the [Belmont's Curse character list](/en/characters/castlevania-belmont-s-curse-character-list/).

**Is Trevor Belmont playable in Castlevania: Belmont's Curse?**

No, Trevor Belmont is not playable in the main campaign based on every official demo, the Steam store description, and the Konami page as of this writing. He appears as an NPC, a flashback figure, and a story anchor for the Belmont family legacy. His visual style is referenced in the Alucard-style costume DLC, but his role in the 1499 story is mentor and father rather than second protagonist.

**Is Sypha Belnades in Castlevania: Belmont's Curse?**

Yes, Sypha Belnades appears in Castlevania: Belmont's Curse as Rose's mother and the source of the Belnades magical lineage. She is not playable but features in flashback scenes, dialogue, and the cathedral stained-glass lore recap. Her grimoire in the Library wing is the in-game justification for Rose's Arcana ability, and her presence is a major reason the magic system feels like a family inheritance rather than a tutorial unlock.

**Is Dracula in Castlevania: Belmont's Curse?**

Yes, Dracula is the central antagonist and final boss of Castlevania: Belmont's Curse. He returns twenty-three years after his 1476 defeat at the hands of Trevor Belmont and his allies, and the Castle manifesting over Paris is tied directly to his resurrection. The encounter itself is covered in the [Dracula boss fight guide](/en/bosses/castlevania-belmont-s-curse-dracula-boss/) for players looking for phase and weakness data.

**Who voices Rose Belmont in Castlevania: Belmont's Curse?**

The English and Japanese voice actors for Rose Belmont have not been confirmed as of this writing. Konami and Evil Empire have announced that the game will ship with full dual-language voice acting matching the franchise tradition, but the specific cast list for Rose, Trevor, Sypha, and the antagonist roster is still pending. Expect reveals in the lead-up to launch, with the credits roll functioning as the final confirmation.
