### Season-Pass vs. Free Update Trade-Off

Players who bought the Midnight Edition got the Alucard costume immediately and a future season pass for the planned expansion waves. The trade-off, then, is whether to lock in early at a discount or wait and buy expansions a la carte. According to community data, the studio's previous titles leaned toward heavy free-update pacing, so the free stack here is expected to remain at parity regardless of how the season pass is structured.

| Option | Cost Timing | Free Content Access | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard edition | Pay once | Yes | Players who only want free updates |
| Midnight Edition | Pay more up front | Yes | Cosmetic hunters + early DLC |
| Season pass only | Mid-launch | Yes | Players who finish the base game first |

The fact that free content is included regardless of edition is the key point — the pass is a discount, not a gate, and that framing is part of why the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse roadmap reads as player-friendly on paper. The community-driven proof point is that the same studio shipped more than 20 free updates for Dead Cells, with paid content layered on top.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When does the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse day-one patch drop?**

The day-one patch is expected to release alongside the global unlock window, and Evil Empire has confirmed it will ship on Steam, Switch, and the Switch 2 version simultaneously. The patch focuses on crash fixes, frame-pacing, and the Reaper boss dash fix reported by community testing. Pre-loaders on Steam Deck OLED are the first to receive the build at the regional unlock time.

**Is the Return to Castlevania crossover free or paid DLC?**

The Return to Castlevania chapter is shipping as a free update, not a paid DLC, and it includes a Beheaded cameo NPC plus a Dead Cells starting trinket for players who link save data. The crossover cosmetic pack layered on top is paid, but every narrative chapter of the roadmap is free. This split is consistent with how Evil Empire handled the Dead Cells crossover cycles in past years.

**What is included in the Carmilla expansion?**

The Carmilla expansion adds a Carpathian chapel biome, a guest Belmont ancestor playable form, 18 new relics, and 5 main bosses plus 2 mini-bosses, all wrapped in a self-contained story arc. The expansion is a single purchase, not a subscription, and is currently slated for the ~6-month window on the public Castlevania: Belmont's Curse roadmap. Difficulty gains a "Bloody" tier above Stalker for the new content.

**Will the Switch 2 version get all of the same updates as Steam?**

Yes — Evil Empire has confirmed that all Castlevania: Belmont's Curse updates will be delivered in parallel across Steam, Switch, and the Switch 2 version, including the Carmilla expansion and every free update in the roadmap. The Switch 2 version is being treated as a peer platform, not a port, which is why the team is keeping a dedicated performance branch active.

**How often will Castlevania: Belmont's Curse free updates drop?**

Based on the studio's prior cadence with Dead Cells, free updates are expected to land roughly every 8-10 weeks, with one paid expansion layered in per quarter. The first free update after launch is the Roses of Atonement quest, followed by the Return to Castlevania crossover and a QoL + accessibility pass. The full Castlevania: Belmont's Curse free update schedule is currently mapped out through the ~9-month anniversary window.
