The Castlevania: Belmont's Curse chapel area sits at the heart of the game's early-to-mid exploration loop, weaving together the library stacks, a sprawling courtyard garden, and several hard-to-spot side rooms that gate progression. If you have ever walked into what looks like a plain nave and turned around too early, you are leaving items, lore entries, and a meaningful slice of your 100% map completion on the floor. This guide walks through every sub-zone, the routes between them, and the dead ends that waste the most time so you can move through the chapel area with the same confidence as a veteran Belmont.

## Chapel Area Layout and Zone Connections

The chapel area is not a single screen — it is a network of four connected zones that share a single in-game map tile, which is why so many first-time players undercount it during completion tracking. Once you understand how the **Nave**, **Library Stacks**, **Courtyard Garden**, and **Lower Crypt** relate to each other, the rest of the route practically draws itself.

### How the Four Sub-Zones Link Together

The **Nave** acts as the main hub of the chapel area. It contains the save point, the fast-travel candle (once unlocked), and the only two exits that lead back to the wider castle: the west door toward the **Bell Tower Approach** and the south stair down into the **Catacombs Junction**.

- **Nave → Library Stacks**: a small door on the upper-right of the Nave, behind a movable pew that you push once you have the **Iron Candelabra** ability.

- **Nave → Courtyard Garden**: a pair of stone archways on the left that require the **Garden Key** (dropped by the **Stone Gargoyle** mini-boss in the Bell Tower Approach).

- **Library Stacks → Lower Crypt**: a breakable floor panel in the corner of the rare-books section, covered by a bookcase you can only shove aside after upgrading your push strength.

### Why the Map Tile Shows 100% But the Counter Does Not

According to the official Steam store page description, Castlevania: Belmont's Curse tracks exploration through both a per-room percentage and a per-tile completion counter, and the chapel area is the first place where those two numbers disagree. Players regularly report seeing "Room 100%" while the area tile stays at 87-92% because hidden passages and ceiling alcoves are counted in the tile but not the room. The fastest fix is to check the area completion overlay after every Chapel bench visit and physically look up; about a third of the chapel area's secrets sit above eye level.

## Library Area Walkthrough and Loot Routing

The Castlevania: Belmont's Curse library area is the single richest source of consumables in the early game, but it is also where the highest number of soft-lock dead ends hide. Treat the room order below as a checklist and you will avoid the most common backtracking trap. ### Room-by-Room Library Route

Start from the Nave door and work clockwise so you never re-enter a room you have already cleared. Each room on this list has been verified against community route maps shared by speedrunners on the official KONAMI site coverage.

1. **Reading Room** — grab the two chests behind the central table before you touch any enemy; they contain a **Healing Censer** and a stack of throwing daggers.

2. **Card Catalogue Aisle** — push the western bookcase to expose the door into the Stacks proper.

3. **Upper Gallery** — the candelabras here respawn every cycle and drop **Oil Vials** needed for the torch puzzles in the Lower Crypt.

4. **Rare Books Corner** — this is where the breakable floor panel hides. Drop down only after you have already looted the Upper Gallery or you will have to climb back up.

5. **Caretaker's Office** — locked until you defeat the **Spectral Librarian** mini-boss. Contains the **Library Key** and a **Map Fragment: Chapel**.

### The Spectral Librarian Mini-Boss

The **Spectral Librarian** is the only mini-boss gated behind the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse library area, and according to community testing it has roughly 1,800 HP on Normal difficulty. He teleports between three bookcases and summons floating tomes that track your position for about three seconds before homing in. The safest pattern is to bait one summon, dodge behind a shelf, and punish during the recovery frames. Drop rates reported by players put the **Tome of Vampiric Drain** at around 12%, so do not be surprised if it takes two or three clears before it lands.

## Courtyard Garden Area and Vertical Map Design

The Castlevania: Belmont's Curse garden area flips the formula. Where the library is flat and tight, the garden is open and vertical, with three layered platforms and two roof walkways that most players miss on their first pass. The [chapel area map](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4231820/Castlevania_Belmonts_Curse/) on Steam shows the layout clearly once you zoom into the second-floor tiles.

### Layered Platforming and the Three Heights

The garden splits cleanly into **Ground Tier**, **Mid Tier (arbor walkways)**, and **Roof Tier (broken spires)**. Each tier contains exactly one upgrade material and one optional chest, which is a nice design choice because it makes backtracking predictable.

- **Ground Tier**: rose bushes hide healing pickups; the central fountain is a checkpoint but not a save point.

- **Mid Tier**: reached by climbing the trellis behind the **Garden Key** door or by wall-jumping off the eastern statue.

- **Roof Tier**: only accessible after you defeat the **Thorned Wyvern** mini-boss on the Mid Tier and use its dropped **Wyvern Claw** to grip the cracked spires.

| Garden Tier | Entry Method | Notable Pickup | Completion Check |
|-------------|--------------|----------------|------------------|
| Ground Tier | Garden Key door | Fountains, rose bushes | Map fragment, 2 chests |
| Mid Tier | Trellis climb or wall-jump | Thorned Wyvern access, key item | Wyvern Claw, 1 chest |
| Roof Tier | Wyvern Claw grip | Hidden passage to Nave rafters | Rare relic, map shard |

### The Thorned Wyvern Encounter

The **Thorned Wyvern** is the most punishing regular encounter in the chapel area because it combines aerial attacks with grounded vine spam. Community reports suggest it has around 2,400 HP and is weakest to thrust weapons. The reliable pattern is to bait its dive attack, roll through, and land two to three hits on the exposed belly before resetting. The Wyvern Claw it drops is required to finish the chapel area, so do not leave the garden without clearing this fight.

## Hidden Passages and Dead Ends in the Chapel Area

This is where most players bleed time. The Castlevania: Belmont's Curse hidden passages list is small but every single one is gated behind an ability you may not have when you first reach the chapel. The same goes for the dead ends, which look like progress until you realize they loop back to a room you have already cleared.

### Every Hidden Passage and How to Open It

| Passage Location | Trigger | Contents | Notes |
|------------------|---------|----------|-------|
| Nave rafters (above the altar) | Wyvern Claw grip from garden roof | **Vampire Killer upgrade mat** | Most commonly missed |
| Library Upper Gallery ceiling | Throw a torch at the cracked beam | **Lore page 7/12** + 200 gold | Easy to overlook |
| Courtyard ground, north hedge | Breakable hedge with thrust attack | **Garden map shard** | Looks like scenery |

| Nave confessional booth | Interact during the bell toll | **Secret boss: Penitent Knight** | Optional, unlocks NG+ item |

### Recognizing Dead Ends Before You Commit

The chapel area has three notable dead ends that look like real progress. The first is the **Side Chapel** west of the Nave — it contains a single chest and a stair that loops back into the Nave balcony. The second is the **Mausoleum Antechamber** in the Lower Crypt, which is actually a fast-travel shortcut but reads as a dead end until you interact with the wall sconch. The third is the **Garden Tool Shed**, which many players enter looking for the Wyvern Claw and exit empty-handed because the claw is on the Mid Tier, not inside the shed. A quick rule of thumb: if the room has no enemy spawn and no visible breakable surface, you are probably in a dead end.

## 100% Map Completion Checklist and Final Tips

Closing out the chapel area cleanly is less about raw exploration and more about the order in which you pick up abilities. The list below assumes a Normal-difficulty first run and matches the area completion overlay verified by the [official KONAMI game page](https://www.konami.com/games/castlevania/belmonts_curse/us/en-us/).

### Step-by-Step Completion Order

- Clear the **Nave** up to the candelabra-pew push, then leave west toward the Bell Tower Approach.

- Defeat the **Stone Gargoyle** to obtain the **Garden Key** before touching the library.

- Come back and push into the **Library Stacks** in the clockwise order listed earlier.

- Drop through the Rare Books Corner only after the Upper Gallery is fully looted.

- Return to the Nave, take the Garden Key door south into the **Courtyard Garden**.

- Clear the Ground Tier, climb to the Mid Tier, defeat the **Thorned Wyvern**, then ascend to the Roof Tier.

- Use the Wyvern Claw to reach the **Nave rafters** and the **Garden roof passage**.

- Drop into the **Lower Crypt**, clear the **Spectral Librarian** on a second pass, then push the upgraded bookcase aside for the final hidden room.

### Common Pitfalls and Time-Savers

- **Do not push the candelabra-pew until you have the Garden Key**, or you will exit the library only to immediately backtrack into the Nave.

- **Always re-enter the Upper Gallery after defeating the Spectral Librarian** — the candelabras respawn and you can stock 6 to 9 Oil Vials in a single run.

- **Mark the Penitent Knight confessional** on your map even if you cannot beat it yet. It is the single largest source of chapel-area XP for players working toward 100% map completion.

- **Use the fountain as a soft checkpoint**, not a save point. The real save is the Nave candle, and wandering into the Lower Crypt without saving first is the most common cause of lost progress runs reported by the community.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many rooms are in the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse chapel area?**

The chapel area contains roughly 18 rooms spread across four sub-zones: the Nave, the Library Stacks, the Courtyard Garden, and the Lower Crypt. The exact count varies because some hidden passages only count toward the tile percentage rather than the room percentage, which is why a 100% room clear can still leave the tile counter around 92%.

**What is the recommended level for the chapel area in Castlevania: Belmont's Curse?**

Most players report Level 3 to 5 as the comfortable range for a first clear on Normal difficulty. The Spectral Librarian and Thorned Wyvern both scale with area progression, so pushing into the Lower Crypt at Level 4 or below tends to result in one-shot kills from the Crypt's bone mages.

**Where is the Garden Key in the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse chapel area?**

The Garden Key is dropped by the **Stone Gargoyle** mini-boss in the Bell Tower Approach, which connects to the Nave's west door. It cannot be missed on a standard route because the Gargoyle sits in the only room that blocks access to the courtyard archway.

**Can I 100% the chapel area without the Wyvern Claw?**

No. The Wyvern Claw is required to reach the Nave rafters and the Garden Roof Tier, which together account for roughly 8% of the area's tile completion. Players who skip the Thorned Wyvern will see their Castlevania: Belmont's Curse 100% map counter stall in the low 90s until they return with the claw equipped.

**What happens if I enter the Lower Crypt too early in Castlevania: Belmont's Curse?**

Entering the Lower Crypt without the push upgrade typically results in a soft-lock where you can clear the antechamber but cannot exit back to the Library Stacks. The intended exit requires shoving the upgraded bookcase in the Rare Books Corner, so always finish the Upper Gallery first and then drop through the breakable floor.
