## What the Alucard Style Costume Includes in the Pre-Order Bonus

Pre-ordering Castlevania: Belmont's Curse through any official storefront — Steam, the Konami store, or the PlayStation and Xbox listings — grants the Alucard Style Costume as a standalone cosmetic bonus. The costume is a full-body skin that reskins Rose Belmont to mirror the iconic half-vampire look worn by Alucard in Castlevania III: Dracula's Curse, the very game this new entry follows 23 years after. The Alucard Style Costume changes Rose's default tunic, cape, hair color, and boots, and it also swaps her idle animation stance into a more composed pose reminiscent of the Alucard fighter stance series fans have come to recognize.

Because the Alucard Style Costume is cosmetic only, it does not alter Rose's base damage, Relic slot count, Arcana card selection, or traversal animations such as the bat-sweep or wall-climb moves. According to the [official Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4231820/Castlevania_Belmonts_Curse/), the bonus item is delivered as an outfit unlock inside Rose's wardrobe menu on first boot, and it persists across save files because it is bound to your account, not your in-game progress. That distinction matters for players who plan multiple New Game+ runs with different Arcana builds, because you will not have to re-pre-order to keep the costume.

## How the Alucard Style Costume Differs From the Midnight Edition

Players often conflate the pre-order Alucard Style Costume with the Midnight Edition bundle, but they are two separate SKUs on the Steam page. The pre-order Alucard Style Costume ships free with any edition of Castlevania: Belmont's Curse, including the standard edition, provided you place the order before launch. The Midnight Edition is a paid upgrade that contains the full game, the digital sound and art gallery, and three additional in-game items beyond the Alucard Style Costume.

Those three bonus items have been confirmed on the [Castlevania: Belmont's Curse product page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4231820/Castlevania_Belmonts_Curse/) and in the Konami press materials. They include a Vampire Killer Relic cosmetic variant, an exclusive Arcana card sleeve, and a set of three vintage-style wallpapers for the main menu. None of these three items are sold separately, and they are not included with the standard pre-order, which is why the Midnight Edition is positioned as the collector-tier option.

- Wait for the game to unlock in your library on October 15, 2026. The exact unlock hour varies by region but is typically around 00:00 to 04:00 local time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Alucard Style Costume carry over to New Game+?**

Yes, the Alucard Style Costume is account-bound rather than save-bound, so it remains available across every New Game+ file. You can switch freely between the costume and any other unlocked outfit without losing access. Players who skip the pre-order window cannot claim the costume retroactively through New Game+ progression, so timing matters.

**What is included in the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse digital sound and art gallery?**

The digital sound and art gallery contains the full 38-track orchestral soundtrack plus roughly 120 concept art pieces covering every region, boss, and NPC. It is delivered as a separate in-game viewer accessible from the extras menu, and it does not require an internet connection after the initial install. The gallery is exclusive to the Midnight Edition.

**Which edition of Castlevania: Belmont's Curse should I buy if I only want the Alucard Style Costume?**

The standard pre-order is the cheapest path if the Alucard Style Costume is your only target, because the bonus ships free with any pre-order tier. The Midnight Edition is justified only if you also want the digital sound and art gallery plus the three Midnight-exclusive items. Post-launch, neither edition includes the Alucard Style Costume.

**Can I get a refund on the Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Midnight Edition if I change my mind?**

Refund eligibility follows Steam's standard two-hour playtime policy, but if you have not launched the game, you can request a full refund up to 14 days after purchase. Once you have redeemed the Alucard Style Costume and launched the game, the refund window narrows to two hours of total playtime. Pre-order cancellations are handled by Steam directly, not by Konami support.
