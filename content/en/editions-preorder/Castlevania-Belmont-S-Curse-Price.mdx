## What Editions of Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Are Available

Konami has confirmed two retail-ready SKUs for Castlevania: Belmont's Curse at launch: the **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Standard Edition** and the **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Midnight Edition**. There is no Collector, no Deluxe, and no physical-only variant in the announced lineup, which keeps the decision matrix tight. Both editions are available digitally on Steam, PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, and Xbox Series X|S, and both editions include the base campaign featuring Rose Belmont's hunt through 15th-century Paris.

The Standard Edition is the cheaper entry point. According to the official Steam store page, it contains the full game and nothing else — no art book, no soundtrack disc, no bonus cosmetic. Players who only care about the campaign and the relic system will not feel shortchanged, because the core experience lives entirely inside the base SKU.

The Midnight Edition sits above the Standard Edition in price and includes three bundled extras: Those are the Digital Sound & Art Gallery, the exclusive Family's Grace Relic with its passive regeneration effect, and a set of in-game consumables that KONAMI has not formally itemized yet.

### Pre-Order Bonus Window

The Alucard Style Costume is gated behind a pre-order window rather than the Midnight Edition SKU itself, which is a common point of confusion. According to the official Steam listing, anyone — Standard or Midnight buyers — who pre-orders the game from an authorized store before launch receives the costume as a **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse pre-order bonus** automatically applied to their account. This means the Midnight Edition is not the only way to obtain Alucard's look, and Standard buyers who pre-order end up with the same cosmetic.

This is also why community discussions in 2026 treat the Midnight Edition as a bundle, not a collector's box. You are paying for the gallery and the three bonus in-game items; the costume is just the cherry on top that everyone gets.

### Tax and Storefront Fees

The displayed **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse price** does not always equal what you pay at checkout. EU buyers add 20–25% VAT at point of sale, and several US states charge 6–10% sales tax depending on the platform's nexus rules. Console storefronts (PSN, eShop, Xbox) typically fold tax into the displayed price, so a $29.99 US figure is genuinely $29.99 on PlayStation, while the same SKU on Steam might show as $32.39 in California with 8% sales tax. Japan, Korea, and Brazil use slightly different consumption-tax models, but the underlying MSRP is the same.

It is worth keeping this in mind when comparing the **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse cost** across platforms on launch day, because a $2–$3 tax spread between Steam and console storefronts can flip the "cheapest" label without any actual price difference. The safest comparison is always the post-checkout total on your own account, because that is the only figure that includes your region's actual tax treatment.

## Standard vs Midnight Edition: Which Is Worth the Price

The $10 USD gap between Standard and Midnight works out to roughly 33% more expensive, and you get four bundled items. Whether that is worth the extra spend depends on how much of the Castlevania franchise's history you care about and whether you replay the campaign more than once.

### For the Casual Single-Playthrough Buyer

If you plan to clear the campaign once and never return, the Standard Edition is the correct call. The Digital Sound & Art Gallery is a bonus you may open once, the Alucard Style Costume is given to every pre-order customer regardless of edition, and the three bonus in-game items tend to be minor consumables or weak starting relics. The **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Standard Edition** delivers the full Rose Belmont story arc, all boss encounters, and the entire relic progression tree, which is what most people actually bought the game for.

### For the Lore and Replay Buyer

The Midnight Edition is the better value for anyone who plans multiple runs, wants to display their relic achievements, or collects KONAMI's art books. The Digital Sound & Art Gallery is the kind of digital museum piece that adds context to every area, and the three bonus in-game items usually include at least one **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse Midnight Edition** exclusive relic that cannot be obtained any other way. Community testing on the closed beta suggests the exclusive relic is the **Family's Grace Relic**, which grants a passive HP regeneration effect that significantly softens the early-game difficulty curve.
